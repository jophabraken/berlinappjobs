Food Stylist (m/f/x)

The role

As a Food Stylist in our Studio, you support the creation of high-quality food visuals across recipe photography and marketing campaigns. Working closely with experienced stylists, cooks, photographers, and Art Directors, you help bring our food to life on set while developing your own styling craft.

This is a hands-on role for someone early in their career who is eager to grow, learn fast, and build strong foundational skills in food and set styling. You will contribute to day-to-day shoot execution, support the team in maintaining high visual standards, and gradually take on more ownership over styling tasks and smaller shoot components.

At HelloFresh, we believe in growing through diverse experiences. If you feel excited by the role but don’t meet every requirement, we still encourage you to apply.

What you’ll do

  • Support food and set styling across recipe shoots, brand campaigns, and digital content
  • Work closely with cooks, photographers, Art Directors, and producers to bring visual concepts to life
  • Prepare and style food elements on set, ensuring consistency, freshness, and visual quality
  • Help manage props, set preparation, and post-shoot organization
  • Learn and apply studio styling standards, contributing to smooth and efficient shoot workflows
  • Stay curious about food trends, styling techniques, and visual inspiration
  • Take direction and feedback constructively to continuously develop your styling skills
  • Support the team in achieving daily shoot goals while maintaining attention to detail and quality

What you’ll bring

  • 2-5 years of experience in food styling, culinary arts, hospitality, or a related creative field
  • A portfolio and visual examples showing your food presentation, styling, or creative work
  • Strong interest in food, photography, and visual storytelling
  • Willingness to learn, take feedback, and grow in a fast-paced studio environment
  • Good communication skills and a collaborative mindset
  • Hands-on attitude, reliability, and strong attention to detail
  • understanding of composition, texture, and color — and how to bring food to life on camera
  • Passion for food, seasonal ingredients, and emerging food styling trends

What we offer

Elevate your lifestyle! Join one of Europe's fastest-growing tech powerhouses in a dynamic phase of expansion.

  • Immerse yourself in a diverse global community of 90+ nationalities.
  • Enjoy a competitive compensation package that goes beyond the norm, with perks like a HelloFresh- subsidized Pension Scheme, Berlin relocation support, and a Hybrid working model.
  • Elevate your lifestyle with exclusive discounts on your weekly HelloFresh box and office meals.
  • Invest in your growth with an annual learning & development budget, German language learning budget, and access to the HelloFresh Academy.
  • Plus, we've got your well-being covered with mental health support, transportation perks, and working-parent-friendly benefits. From our 24/7 gym access,wellbeing platforms like Headspace and Spill, to sabbatical leave options, HelloFresh is not just a workplace; it's a lifestyle of perks and possibilities!

About HelloFresh

HelloFresh is on a mission to change the way people eat, forever!

Since our 2011 founding in Europe’s vibrant tech hub, Berlin, we’ve become the world's leading meal kit provider, delivering to over 4.2 million households worldwide in 15 countries across 3 continents. 

We will encourage you to make an immediate impact in your area of work as well as empower you to grow your career with us.
