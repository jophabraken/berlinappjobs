Our goal is to have a solar system on every roof, a battery in every home, and an electric vehicle in every garage. Enpal makes this possible with an integrated, end-to-end solution for decentralized energy—from solar panels and battery storage to EV chargers, smart meters, and heat pumps.

At the heart of it all is Enpal.One+, our AI-powered platform that intelligently connects thousands of energy systems and optimizes electricity consumption and grid feed-in in real time.

Are you ready to work on solutions that deliver more than promises—solutions that improve the quality of life for thousands of households every day? At Enpal, the work you do today helps deliver clean energy tomorrow and creates a lasting impact on the way we generate, manage, and use energy.

Your Tasks

  • You design and implement automations that replace manual processes with scalable workflows

  • You work with tools such as Cursor, Snowflake, Airtable, Power BI, Make, and n8n

  • You build autonomous agent-based systems for pricing, market analysis, and demand forecasting

  • You independently manage projects from analysis and solution design through implementation, ensuring measurable business impact

  • You understand how to design robust data architectures and maintain clear, comprehensive documentation

Your Profile

  • You are currently studying Business Informatics, Computer Science, Data Science, Machine Learning, or a related field

  • You have already gained initial experience in automation, data analytics, or artificial intelligence

  • You enjoy working with tools such as Cursor, Airtable, Make, n8n, Power BI, Snowflake, SQL, or similar technologies

  • You have a strong analytical mindset, work in a structured and solution-oriented way, and enjoy solving complex problems pragmatically

  • You take ownership, work independently, and proactively drive initiatives forward.

  • Good English skills are sufficient, German is a plus

If you are interested even if you do not meet all the requirements, apply anyway! We look forward to discovering your potential—regardless of whether your experience fulfills every single requirement. ☀️

Work at Germany’s first green unicorn – actively shape the solar energy transition.

The sun shines all over the world – At Enpal you work with a highly motivated, diverse team of over 65 nationalities.

Grown-up business & startup spirit – In our modern office in Berlin-Friedrichshain you’ll find everything your heart desires, from height-adjustable desks and a ping-pong table to fully stocked drink fridges and barista coffee.

Your kick-start at Enpal – onboarding day with welcome bag, buddy program, and a team that truly supports you.

Stay up to date & help shape things – transparent all-hands meetings, short decision-making paths, and an open feedback culture, because without mistakes there is no progress.

The energy transition only works together – at Enpal you can expect a legendary team spirit and unforgettable team and company events.

Your extra boost – 29 + 2 vacation days, discounted Wellhub membership, and corporate benefits. Everything you need to balance work and life well.
