About the opportunity

As an IT Solutions Engineer (Google Cloud) within N26 Internal IT, you will be the primary architect, engineer, and guardian of our Google Workspace and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) corporate ecosystem.

We aren’t looking for traditional system administrators. We need a modern, engineering-focused professional who treats our Google environment as a programmable platform. You will bridge the gap between corporate productivity, identity management, and cloud infrastructure—ensuring our global workforce is secure, highly automated, and empowered by next-generation AI integrations.

In this role, you will:

  • Infrastructure & Governance: Manage the full lifecycle of our enterprise Google Workspace organization. Design and maintain complex GCP project structures, organizational units (OUs), Cloud Identity, and Chrome Enterprise across the company.

  • Automation & Development: Eliminate manual overhead by building robust, custom automation workflows. Leverage Google Apps Script, Python, and REST APIs to seamlessly integrate Workspace with internal tools, HRIS platforms, and Identity providers.

  • Next-Gen Productivity: Lead the rollout, adoption, and ongoing optimization of Gemini Enterprise, driving organizational productivity through emerging AI capabilities.

  • Security & Compliance: Design and enforce Data Loss Prevention (DLP) rules, Context-Aware Access (CAA), and secure BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) policies that balance "Security first" with "Employee Experience."

  • eDiscovery & Compliance: Serve as the technical owner for Google Vault. Define and manage data retention policies, execute precise legal holds, and lead matter-based exports to meet strict financial regulatory and audit requirements.

What you need to be successful:

  • Google Workspace & GCP Expertise: Deep, hands-on experience administering Google Workspace at an enterprise scale, combined with solid foundational knowledge of GCP (IAM hierarchies, Service Accounts, VPCs, and Organization Policies).

  • Automation Mindset: Proven track record of automating repetitive IT workflows using Python, JavaScript, or Google Apps Script, and interacting with RESTful APIs.

  • Infrastructure as Code Skills: Practical experience (or strong familiarity) using Terraform to manage SaaS or cloud environments.

  • Security & Identity Focus: Deep understanding of modern identity management, SAML/OIDC protocols, and Zero Trust security architectures (specifically Google’s Context-Aware Access and BeyondCorp models).

  • Chrome Enterprise Management: Proven experience configuring Chrome as a secure enterprise browser, including the deployment of Chrome Enterprise Premium security controls.

  • Analytical & Compliance-Minded: Familiarity with compliance standards (e.g., GDPR, SOC2) and the operational workflows of Google Vault for legal and audit discovery.

  • Collaborative Spirit: Strong communication skills with the ability to translate complex technical requirements into user-friendly solutions for a diverse global team.

Nice to Have

  • Professional Certifications: Google Cloud Professional Collaboration Engineer, Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect, or equivalent security certifications.

  • Fintech Experience: Experience working in highly regulated industries (banking, fintech, or healthcare) with stringent data governance requirements.

What’s in it for you:

  • Accelerate your career growth by joining one of Europe’s most talked about disruptors 🚀.
  • Employee benefits that range from a competitive personal development budget, work from home budget, discounts to fitness & wellness memberships, language apps and public transportation. 
  • As an N26 employee you will have access to a Premium subscription on your personal N26 bank account. As well as subscriptions for friends and family members.    
  • Additional day of annual leave for each year of service.  
  • A high degree of autonomy and access to cutting edge technologies - all while working with a friendly team of peers of diverse nationalities, experiences, and backgrounds. 
  • A relocation package with visa support for those who need it.

Who we are

N26 has reimagined banking for today’s digital world. Technology and design empower everything we do and it’s how we are building the global banking platform the world loves to use.

We've eliminated physical branches, paperwork, and hidden fees for an elegant digital experience and supreme savings. Giving people the power to live and bank their way is what gets us out of bed in the morning and inspires the work that we do. 

We are headquartered in Berlin with offices in multiple cities across Europe, including Vienna and Barcelona, and a 1,500-strong team of more than 80 nationalities.
