Marketing Technology Developer (m/f/d)*

Bloomwell GmbHOffice Frankfurt/HybridFull-timePosted 2026-07-03

Deine Vision

You don’t want to choose between marketing and engineering, you want to own both? Then join Bloomwell. We are one of the leading health tech companies in medical cannabis, with the mission to make access to therapy as simple as possible for patients. Digital, patient centric and fast growing.
As Marketing Technology Developer you are the bridge between our marketing and engineering teams. You split your time between implementing marketing requirements such as tracking, integrations and analytics, and building the backend infrastructure that powers our marketing capabilities. You work AI first, take full ownership of your domain, and make sure that what you ship actually delivers business value, not just closes a ticket.

Deine Aufgaben

  • Implement tracking events and SDK integrations across our Flutter app for Android, iOS and Web
  • Configure and maintain our marketing technology stack including Adjust, Usercentrics, Google Tag Manager and social platform integrations such as Meta and TikTok
  • Ensure compliance with platform specific tracking restrictions such as iOS App Tracking Transparency, Android privacy changes and GDPR
  • Build custom HubSpot workflow integrations via TypeScript endpoints
  • Maintain and extend our in house tracking service built with TypeScript and Express
  • Build new backend services for marketing needs such as notification systems or segmentation engines
  • Design and implement APIs for marketing automation workflows and own them from requirement to production deployment
  • Prioritize your own roadmap and work closely with marketing to prevent assumption driven development
  • Use AI coding assistants and automation tools to spend less time on boilerplate and more on high value problem solving

The position is initially limited to 18 months. This is in accordance with our internal governance and planning principles, under which we structure employment relationships for a defined period of time initially. If performance is as expected, there is generally the prospect of a long-term continuation of the employment relationship. A possible extension will be discussed well in advance of the end of the fixed-term period.

Dein Profil

  • Several years of solid, hands-on experience of professional software development experience with strong TypeScript and Node.js skills as your primary stack
  • Experience with Flutter or willingness to learn it, since you will work on our mobile and web app
  • Solid knowledge of tracking and analytics tools such as Adjust, Usercentrics, Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Firebase Analytics, as well as social platform integrations
  • Understanding of platform tracking restrictions including iOS ATT, Android privacy changes and web cookie or GDPR requirements
  • Comfortable working AI first with tools such as Cursor, Copilot or MCP integrations to automate repetitive work
  • Ownership mentality, you prioritize ruthlessly, ship to production and validate real business value rather than just completing tickets
  • Nice to have: HubSpot CRM experience, Webflow, notification services such as push, email or SMS, background in marketing technology or growth engineering, previous startup experience
  • Very good English skills, German skills are a plus
  • You live in or close by Frankfurt

Warum wir?

You get to actively shape things from day one, in a growing market, with flat hierarchies and real room to decide. No slow moving structures, just fast paths and an open culture where your ideas count.
This is what you can expect:
  • Ownership from day one with a direct line to management
  • Flexible working hours and hybrid work, three days a week on site in our Frankfurt office, the rest remote
  • eGym Wellpass for health and fitness
  • Job-Bike
  • Fresh fruit, snacks and drinks in our Frankfurt office near the Messe
  • Regular team events and a vibrant company culture

*We use the gender neutral abbreviation (m/w/d) to indicate that this position is open to all genders.
