Our goal is to have a solar system on every roof, a storage unit in every house, and an electric car in every garage. Enpal makes this possible with an integrated total solution for decentralized energy—from solar systems and battery storage to wall boxes, smart meters, and heat pumps. At the heart of it all is our AI-powered platform Enpal.One+, which intelligently connects thousands of systems and efficiently optimizes electricity procurement and feed-in on the energy market.

Are you ready for solutions that are more than just a promise and bring real quality of life to thousands of households every day? What you create at Enpal will deliver clean electricity tomorrow and bring about lasting change in how we use energy.

What you’ll do

As a Lead Expert in Group Accounting, you will take ownership of complex consolidation and reporting topics, contribute to Enpal’s IFRS and HGB consolidated financial statements, and work closely with accounting teams and senior Finance stakeholders across the Group.

  • You take end-to-end ownership of complex Group Accounting and consolidation topics and play a key role in preparing Enpal’s consolidated financial statements under IFRS and HGB.

  • You analyze and validate Group financial results, challenge inconsistencies and unusual developments, and work with local accounting teams to resolve issues before consolidation.

  • You perform and review key consolidation activities, including intercompany reconciliations and eliminations, equity consolidation and Group-level adjustments.

  • You perform analytical reviews of P&L and balance sheet movements, identify significant variances and develop a strong understanding of the key drivers behind the Group’s financial results.

  • You prepare and improve Group-level analyses, reconciliations and reporting schedules and ensure consistency between underlying accounting records, consolidation data and reported Group figures.

  • You manage and coordinate IFRS and HGB Group audits, acting as a central point of contact for external auditors and driving audit requests and open items through to resolution.

  • You work closely with GL Accounting, Technical Accounting, FP&A, Tax and other Finance teams to understand business developments and their impact on Group reporting.

  • You support the integration of new entities and changes in the Group structure and contribute to the continuous improvement and scalability of Enpal’s consolidation and reporting processes.

What you’ll bring

  • You have a degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration or a comparable field and several years of relevant experience in Group Accounting, consolidation, financial reporting or audit, ideally in a complex or international organization.

  • You have strong IFRS and HGB knowledge and hands-on experience preparing consolidated financial statements within complex Group structures.

  • You have a solid understanding of consolidation topics, including intercompany eliminations, equity consolidation and Group-level adjustments.

  • You have experience analyzing Group financial results and can connect accounting and consolidation data with the underlying business developments.

  • You combine strong technical accounting expertise with a pragmatic, hands-on mindset and can translate complex requirements into practical solutions.

  • You take ownership of complex topics, work independently and proactively identify issues, investigate root causes and drive them through to resolution.

  • You are confident working with senior stakeholders and coordinating across different Accounting and Finance functions. Experience with LucaNet or another consolidation/reporting system is a strong advantage.

  • You communicate confidently in English; German is a strong advantage, particularly given the HGB reporting and audit responsibilities. Experience in Audit or Accounting Advisory.

Are you interested even if you don't meet all the requirements? Apply anyway! We look forward to discovering your potential, regardless of whether your experience meets every single requirement. ☀️
