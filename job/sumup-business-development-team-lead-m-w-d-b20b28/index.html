Business Development Team Lead

EU GTM | Cologne, Germany

About the team 🙌

SumUp's SDR function is one of the fastest-growing parts of our European sales engine — and we need a Team Lead in Cologne who's ready to own it. You'll be the person your team looks to every day: coaching, calling it out, keeping energy high, and driving results that actually move the needle. This isn't a seat-warmer role. You'll shape how your team operates, build habits that stick, and create a culture where people genuinely want to perform. If you've been a top SDR and you're ready to lead — this is your moment. 👇

What you'll do

  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Lead and coach a team of SDRs in Cologne — drive performance across outbound activity, qualified appointments, and pipeline contribution

  • ☀️ Run daily rituals that actually matter — standups, call reviews, 1:1s — keeping the team sharp and motivated

  • 📊 Track KPIs like a hawk — spot performance gaps early and act fast with the right support

  • 🤝 Partner with Sales Managers, RevOps, and the Outbound Growth Lead to sharpen lead quality, targeting, and sequencing

  • 🚀 Onboard new SDRs and ramp them fast — build the habits that turn new hires into top performers

  • 📝 Help build and improve outbound playbooks, cadences, and messaging — iterate quickly on what works

  • 🌱 Develop your team's careers — identify high performers and create real pathways into full-cycle sales roles

  • 📢 Report on team performance to senior leadership with clarity — trends, blockers, opportunities

You'll be great for this role if…

  • You've been a top-performing SDR or inside sales rep and you're ready to bring others up with you

  • You've already built or managed a team before — you know what it takes and you're not starting from zero

  • You know what great outbound looks like — from prospecting and sequencing to objection handling — and you can teach it

  • You're a hands-on, energetic leader who brings the heat even on tough days

  • You're analytical and decisive — you use data to diagnose issues and make clear calls

  • You communicate with clarity whether you're coaching a rep live or briefing senior stakeholders

  • You thrive in evolving environments where your initiative actually shapes how things get built

  • You speak German fluently and have a working level of English for internal comms
