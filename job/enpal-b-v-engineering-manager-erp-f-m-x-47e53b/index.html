Engineering Manager ERP (f/m/x)

Enpal B.V.BerlinFull-timePosted 2026-08-11

Our goal is to have a solar system on every roof, a storage unit in every house, and an electric car in every garage. Enpal makes this possible with an integrated total solution for decentralized energy—from solar systems and battery storage to wall boxes, smart meters, and heat pumps. At the heart of it all is our AI-powered platform Enpal.One+, which intelligently connects thousands of systems and efficiently optimizes electricity procurement and feed-in on the energy market.

Are you ready for solutions that are more than just a promise and bring real quality of life to thousands of households every day? What you create at Enpal will deliver clean electricity tomorrow and bring about lasting change in how we use energy.

What you'll do

Enpal is transforming its ERP landscape from a traditional D365 F&O setup into an Agentic Enterprise, a clean D365 core with an AI-native execution layer on top. AI-native product squads, organized by E2E process domain (D2O, O2C, P2P, SCE, R2R, MDG), replace traditional workstream towers. Each squad includes a Business Intent Owner (BIO), a Technology Orchestrator, Context Engineers, and Harness Engineers. This role sits in ERP Engineering and is a core contributor to building the agentic operating model that will power Enpal at scale.

As Engineering Manager ERP, you lead the team responsible for the technical heart of Enpal's agentic enterprise: Microsoft Dynamics 365 F&O and the AI-native execution layer being built on top of it. You are both a technical leader and a people manager. You steer the transition from a heavily customized, fragmented D365 landscape to a clean-core, max-standard platform and you build the engineering capability to deliver agentic extensions, integrations, and agent harnesses at the speed the transformation demands.

TECHNICAL LEADERSHIP & ARCHITECTURE

  • Define and execute the ERP engineering strategy: transition from a customized D365 landscape to a clean-core, max-standard platform with agentic extensions built within clean-core guardrails

  • Evaluate the current D365 F&O landscape: architecture, customization debt, integration patterns, performance, data quality, and adjacent system dependencies and define the transformation path forward

  • Own the technical architecture for the agentic layer: agent harnesses, MCP/A2A integration standards, runtime pipelines, guardrails, and monitoring infrastructure

  • Ensure all D365 customizations are reduced to the minimum, only where legally required or business-critical, and that every extension decision is tested against clean-core fit, cost efficiency, and platform alignment

DELIVERY & ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

  • Oversee end-to-end D365 F&O development and integrations: clean technical design, code quality, documentation standards, and release governance

  • Implement strong DevOps practices and CI/CD pipelines for smooth, predictable releases and deployments, including agentic build, test, and ship cycles

  • Design and scale integrations with Enpal's broader tech ecosystem: CRM (Salesforce), IoT, Azure, Snowflake, Aryza, Kyriba, and the Microsoft AI platform (Copilot Studio, Azure AI Foundry, Microsoft Fabric)

  • Ensure the D365 core is the trusted system of record: deterministic ledger, traceable decisions, full audit trail, the foundation that makes agentic operations trustworthy

PEOPLE LEADERSHIP & TEAM BUILDING

  • Lead, mentor, and grow a team of ERP developers and engineers, including hiring, onboarding, and developing talent as Enpal scales across 30+ legal entities

  • Foster a culture of engineering excellence and ownership: AI-native delivery mindset, clean-core discipline, and continuous improvement

  • Balance innovation, scalability, and operational stability across a fast-growth environment with continuous business unit onboarding

What you'll bring

  • Proven experience as an Engineering Manager, Senior ERP Architect, or Technical Lead with strong people leadership in a modern engineering environment

  • In-depth expertise in Microsoft Dynamics 365 F&O or Dynamics AX/AX2012, including customization, integration, and architecture

  • Proven experience conducting independent ERP technical assessments and translating findings into actionable roadmaps

  • Deep expertise in D365 F&O architecture, LCS, Azure DevOps, and integration design

  • Hands-on background in software engineering (C#, X++, .NET, Azure, or similar cloud-based stacks)

  • Experience with or strong interest in agentic AI engineering: MCP/A2A standards, agent harness design, LLM integration, or AI-native delivery models

  • Deep understanding of core ERP process domains (Finance, Supply Chain, Procurement) and the ability to translate business complexity into clean technical architecture

  • Experience building and leading teams that deliver scalable, stable, and high-performing systems in a fast-growth environment

  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills across technical and non-technical domains

  • Fluent in English; German is a plus

Are you interested even if you don't meet all the requirements? Apply anyway! We look forward to discovering your potential—regardless of whether your experience meets every single requirement. ☀️

Why this role matters

Enpal is pioneering the Agentic ERP: a clean D365 core with an AI-native execution layer delivered at pace. The Engineering Manager is the technical owner of that platform. You are not maintaining a legacy system; you are building the engineering foundation for an agent-operated enterprise. The quality, speed, and discipline of your team's delivery directly determines whether the transformation succeeds.
