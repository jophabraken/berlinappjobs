Explore a new world of possibilities

At HomeToGo, we’re on a mission to make incredible accommodation easily accessible for everyone. Our team is united by a passion for travel, driven by innovation and impact, and guided by open-minded leaders at every level. Our commitment to recognizing and empowering exceptional talent is the foundation of our growth. An amazing journey of opportunity with an international team lies ahead - join us!


Your future contribution

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced IT Administrator to oversee and maintain our IT infrastructure, ensuring the smooth operation of IT services across the organization. This role requires a deep understanding of Networking (Juniper), IT Service Management (ITSM), infrastructure maintenance, and high-level technical support, along with experience in GitHub administration. While a basic understanding of DevOps practices is beneficial, this is primarily an IT administration role focused on system reliability, security, and efficiency.


How you’ll add value

  • Own the network infrastructure and ensure operational readiness, high availability, and continuous improvement.

  • Lead IT service management processes, ensuring compliance with ITIL best practices and SLAs.

  • Provide high-level technical support, manage on- and offboarding, and resolve escalated IT issues in a timely manner. 

  • Manage and maintain on-premise and cloud-based IT infrastructure, ensuring system performance, security, and availability.

  • Administer Okta, Google Workspace and Atlassian and ensure seamless user authentication, access control, and security compliance.

  • Continuously improve workflows to enhance user experience and security.


You’ll be successful in this role if you

  • Have 4+ years of experience in IT Administration, Networking, and end-user Support, and IT Service Management.

  • Bring a strong networking background (routing, switching, VPNs, network security).

  • Demonstrate proven experience in ITSM, high-level troubleshooting, and infrastructure maintenance.

  • Have hands-on experience with Google Workspace, Okta, Jira, and Confluence administration.

  • Collaborate with DevOps and engineering teams to ensure IT infrastructure aligns with development needs.

  • Bring familiarity with AWS, Kubernetes, and automation tools (a plus).

  • Communicate clearly, with strong written and verbal proficiency in English.

  • Bring strong problem-solving and analytical skills, paired with a proactive mindset. 

  • Prioritize and manage multiple tasks in a dynamic environment.


What we offer

  • AI-first culture: Leverage a world-class AI ecosystem with Google Enterprise+, Slack AI, and SOTA model access via Copilot, Claude or LiteLLM. By using our custom agents for automation and knowledge management, you’ll become a first-class adopter of the AI workflows defining industry best practices.

  • Flexible hybrid work: Work remotely up to 50% of the year, including up to 63 days of "work from anywhere" within the EU and UK.*

  • Competitive compensation: Market-aligned salary plus stock options for eligible roles.

  • Generous time off: 27 vacation days to start, increasing with tenure to up to 32 days.

  • Dog-friendly offices: Bring your four-legged friend along – our offices welcome well-behaved dogs.

  • Relocation & visa support: Full visa sponsorship and relocation support for international hires joining our Berlin office.*

  • Growth & development: German classes, masterclasses, and cross-team mentoring.

  • Commuter & mobility perks: Monthly subsidy for the Deutschland-Ticket and tax-advantaged bike leasing via salary conversion.

  • Wellbeing & fitness: Mental health support resources and subsidized sports memberships.

  • Travel & lifestyle perks: Exclusive discounts via HomeToGo_Friends, Travel Industry Card deals, and 20% off Union Berlin fan gear.

  • Climate responsibility: We practice responsible stewardship daily and embed ESG at the heart of our operations, while collaborating with Plan A to stay accountable for our carbon footprint and environmental impact. 


*Certain conditions apply.
