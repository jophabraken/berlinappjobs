Internship Strategy (f/m/d) - Autumn cohort

Was wir tun

At getquin, we’re on a mission to empower everyone to build wealth.

Founded in 2020, we started with a simple idea: help people see their entire financial life in one place, so they can make more informed decisions.

Today, getquin has grown into one of the largest wealth platforms globally, already helping over half a million users manage and grow their wealth. Our platform combines analytics, tools and community to make wealth-building accessible for everyone, not just the experts.

After launching in Germany, we’ve started expanding across Europe, with a focus on growing our user base, building valuable new features and helping even more people take control of their financial future.

If you're excited to help shape the future of personal wealth management, join us. We're just getting started.

Was du tun wirst

As an intern in Strategy, you will work on the further development and growth of our company.
  • With your analytical skills and your hands-on mentality, you support our Chief of Staff and the Founders in strategic and operational activities.
  • Research, analysis and presentations are an important part of our day-to-day business and support the development of new ideas and projects.
  • You use data to question the status quo and provide impetus for the constant further development of our business areas. 
  • There is no "typical day" for you, as your tasks are very varied and you get impressions of all areas of the company.

Was wir suchen

  • You are a student or graduate from a top university with a technical or business background.
  • You have already gained initial professional experience in a competitive environment (e.g. successful start-up, consulting, investment banking).
  • You are characterized by your independence and hands-on mentality.
  • Your analytical and communication skills are excellent.
  • You can confidently communicate in English and German. 
  • You are available for this internship for at least three months and starting 1st December (not later). The location of the internship is our office in Berlin Mitte.
  • Visa support ist not provided for this role. 

Warum bei getquin?

We’re a fast-growing B2C startup, and here, your work has real impact.
From day one, you'll take ownership of your domain, ship features end-to-end, and help shape the direction of a product used across Europe. We value initiative, ownership and creativity - not layers of process.
