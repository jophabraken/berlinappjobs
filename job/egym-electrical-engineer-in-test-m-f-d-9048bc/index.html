Are you ready to revolutionize the fitness and health industry and contribute to a healthier society? Do you want to enhance our EGYM hardware products? Join our Electrical Engineering team in Munich as an Electrical Engineer in Test (m/f/d). You will lead industrial testing solutions, focusing on PCBA validation and test systems, while collaborating with software and mechanical engineering teams. Apply now and be part of our innovative journey!

Your daily workout

  • Electronics Testing: You lead the validation phase of PCBAs and other electromechanical assemblies, including the definition of test protocols as well as the research and implementation of standards

  • Test System Design: You design and develop test systems as an integral part of your daily work. Together with our Mechanical and Software teams, you create new testing procedures and systems for DUTs ranging from single components to complex mechatronic assemblies. You take ownership from conceptualization and prototyping to full deployment

  • Data Management: You handle the created measurement data in a systematic way and support in developing a global solution for testing data management and evaluation

  • Quality Assurance: You position yourself at the forefront of EGYM’s quality assurance for electronic components and enable the Quality department to apply standard testing procedures for series production

  • Documentation: You document your technical work, manage your tasks, and share your progress transparently. You plan your own projects and supervise their timelines in accordance with company targets

Your fitness level

  • Degree in Electrical Engineering You have a degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics or an equivalent educational background

  • Professional Experience: You have at least 3 years of professional experience in an engineering environment and are used to product development procedures

  • Electronics Debugging: You know your way around electronic debugging, laboratory measurements, and signal analysis

  • Software Skills: You have experience with software development or scripting solutions necessary for efficiently testing and debugging electronics

  • Technical Knowledge: You can prove knowledge of the following (as a plus):

    • Embedded software development (C, C++) & version control (Git)

    • Basic knowledge of UNIX shell & network stack

    • Automated industrial production environments

    • GUI development and scripting languages (Labview / Teststand)

    • Signal integrity assessment and simulations

    • EMC precompliance methods

    • Management tools such as Jira

Your training goal for your first 6 months

  • Ownership: You take ownership of an existing testing system and manage its next development steps

  • Product development: You accompany a PCBA through the validation phase and apply the methods from our testing catalogue

  • Knowledge Sharing: You share your knowledge and experience of electronics design, system design and testing methodologies

  • Product Understanding: You understand the EGYM product portfolio, our unique selling points and processes in hardware development

  • Production Environment: You experience our production and assembly facilities hands-on and learn about the challenges and opportunities

  • Feedback Culture : You become part of EGYM’s lively and open working environment and actively participate in our feedback culture

The equipment we provide

  • Modern & International: Be part of an international company culture where talent and passion are valued, heard and included in the decision-making process

  • Work-Life Balance: Benefit from flexible working hours, hybrid workplace model and 30 days of vacation per year

  • Mentoring Program: Exchange knowledge and grow together across teams and locations through our self-organized mentoring platform

  • Fit & Healthy: Train for free with EGYM Wellpass and use our in-house gym equipped with EGYM Smart Strength and Smart Flex machines

  • Employee Discounts: Have access to a variety of great discounted offers, from fashion to leisure, through our employee benefits portal

  • Bike Leasing: Stay active by taking advantage of our bike leasing offer for your commute or leisure activities

  • Subsidized Pension: Profit from our employer-subsidized pension

  • Flex Budget: Use €60 per month flexibly for public transportation and a meal subsidy

  • Rooftop Terrace: Enjoy your lunch breaks or an after work drinks with your colleagues on our roof terrace when in the office

About EGYM

Warning regarding phishing emails: Please be aware that all official EGYM recruitment communication is sent exclusively from jobs@egym.com. If you receive a suspicious message from any other domain, please ignore it and do not share personal information if in doubt.

EGYM is a global fitness technology leader, providing fitness and health facilities with intelligent workout solutions. EGYM makes exercising smarter and more efficient with its comprehensive suite of connected gym equipment and digital products that integrate seamlessly with 3rd-party-hard- and software. The result is a fully connected training experience that drives measurable business and health outcomes on and off the training floor.

EGYM also offers subscription-based corporate fitness- and wellness solutions built on a combination of gym-access and EGYM fitness programs that directly target costly chronic conditions and boost employee health, leading to higher productivity and well-being.

EGYM's global headquarters are in Munich, Germany, with North American offices in Denver, Colorado.
