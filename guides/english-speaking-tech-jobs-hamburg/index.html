English-Speaking Tech Jobs in Hamburg: Companies That Hire in English

ENUpdated 2026-09-22

Hamburg is Germany's second city and a serious tech hub: games, e-commerce, media and insurance. Of the 202 open roles at Hamburg app companies on this board, about 121, or 60 percent, are advertised in English. Data as of September 2026.

Companies in Hamburg that hire in English

  • Intelligent Apps: the company behind the FREENOW ride-hailing app (part of Lyft since 2025), around 24 English-language roles.
  • aestimium: the team behind the empfohlen.de app, around 19 roles, mostly senior engineering and data.
  • Stillfront Supremacy: game studio (Supremacy: Call of War), around 11 roles.
  • ABOUT YOU: fashion e-commerce, around 10 roles.
  • LOTTO24 and Tipp24: lottery apps, several roles with remote options.
  • InnoGames: Forge of Empires, with published salary ranges.
  • Star Finanz (the S-pushTAN and Sparkasse app maker), freenet, Rexx Systems, DER SPIEGEL and the DAK mobile team: a mix of English and German roles.

Which roles are open in English

Engineering and data lead: backend engineers (Go, PHP), fullstack developers, Android developers, data analysts and heads of engineering. Hamburg also has an unusually high number of product roles for its size, from product managers for AI search to technical product owners. Game studios add game designers, economy designers and analysts.

Where German is still expected

Hamburg's traditional employers (insurers, publishers, retailers) mostly advertise in German even for digital roles. At HanseMerkur, SIGNAL IDUNA and many media houses most roles are advertised in German. If you do not speak German, focus on the game studios, e-commerce and the venture-backed companies above.

Hamburg vs Berlin and Munich

Hamburg has fewer open roles than Berlin (202 vs 779) and a lower English share (60 vs 75 percent), but a strong games scene and many established, profitable companies. Compared to Munich, rents are somewhat lower and the job market is more mixed between international and traditional German employers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I work in tech in Hamburg without German?
Yes, at many companies. About 60 percent of roles at Hamburg app companies are advertised in English, especially at game studios, e-commerce and venture-backed companies.
Which Hamburg companies hire in English?
Intelligent Apps, aestimium, Stillfront Supremacy, ABOUT YOU, LOTTO24, Tipp24 and InnoGames, among others.
Is Hamburg good for game developers?
Yes. Hamburg is Germany's second games hub after Berlin, home to InnoGames, Goodgame Studios, Stillfront Supremacy and Playa Games.
